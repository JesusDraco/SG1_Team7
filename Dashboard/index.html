Neighborhood Energy Simulation Dashboard

Why this dashboard matters

This dashboard shows how different energy management strategies affect a neighborhood’s dependence on the grid, self-consumption, costs, and battery usage. The goal is not only to visualize data, but to explain which strategy creates the most balanced and resilient local energy behavior.

By Household Type

This chart compares household groups using the selected metric, such as self-consumed energy, generation, consumption, cost, or battery discharge. It is useful for identifying which household profile performs better under the current strategy and season, and for comparing how different residential patterns behave in the neighborhood.

Production vs Consumption by Wealth

Average generation and consumption by wealth level. This chart compares average generation and average consumption across wealth levels. It helps show whether certain wealth groups consume more energy than they produce, which groups are closer to energy balance, and how socioeconomic differences affect local energy behavior.

Household Scatter

This chart compares each household’s self-consumption ratio against its net cost. It helps identify which households are operating efficiently, which ones still depend heavily on the grid, and which groups combine higher local energy usage with lower cost. Bubble size represents total household consumption.

Timeseries by Household Type

This chart shows how electricity consumption changes over time for each household type. It helps compare behavioral patterns between groups, identify which type tends to consume more energy, and detect when demand peaks happen across the selected time scale. Each color represents a different household type.

Battery Utilization

Average battery discharge by household type. This chart compares average battery discharge across household types. It helps show which groups rely more on stored energy, how strongly batteries contribute to covering demand, and which household profiles make greater use of storage under the selected operating strategy.

Energy Surplus vs Deficit

Net load aggregated by selected timescale. This chart shows net load over the selected time scale, highlighting periods of surplus and deficit. It is useful for understanding when local generation is enough to cover demand and when the neighborhood still requires support from storage or the grid.
